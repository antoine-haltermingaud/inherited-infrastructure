# Rejected ideas — 04-EcsVsK8s

- Consolidate onto EKS as the default target ("industry standard, less lock-in") — rejected: with a handful of engineers and no ops team, the k8s platform tax (control-plane upgrades, add-on matrix, version treadmill) is the deciding cost; EKS survives only as a discovery-dependent branch.
- Pure "it depends" non-position — rejected: requirements demand a position, and the stated constraints are decisive enough to commit to a default.
- Recommend keeping both platforms as steady state — rejected: two deploy pipelines / patching models / on-call knowledge sets is the most expensive redundancy a team this size can carry.
- Include a migration plan in the deliverable — rejected: out of scope for one paragraph; a single "gradual, never big-bang" clause suffices.
