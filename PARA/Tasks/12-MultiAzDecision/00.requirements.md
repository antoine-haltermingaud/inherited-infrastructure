## Acceptance criteria

- A one-page decision record: monthly cost delta, realistic AZ/instance failure likelihood, estimated cost-of-outage, recommendation, sign-off from whoever owns the budget.
- If YES: enabled off-peak (online operation, brief performance impact during standby sync), verified failover documentation for the team.
- If NO: revisit trigger defined (user growth threshold or first real AZ event) and recorded — "no" without a trigger is drift.

## Constraints

- Decision recorded even if the answer is no. Likely "yes" at this user count per REVIEW.md — but priced, not reflexed.
