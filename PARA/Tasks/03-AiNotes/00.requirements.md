# Requirements — 03-AiNotes

Deliverable: `AI-NOTES.md` at repo root. A few short paragraphs.

## Acceptance criteria

- States how AI was actually used across the exercise, concretely.
- At least one concrete place where an AI suggestion was corrected or discarded, with the reason.
- The disagreement is real and specific — a named suggestion and why it was wrong *here*.

## Constraints

- Short. Budget ~20 minutes.
