# Rejected ideas — 11-TlsIngress

- Ship TLS in week one with a made-up domain — the cert is trivial, the domain decision isn't mine to make alone; a rename later is a second consumer migration.
- Self-signed or ALB default cert — clients would have to disable verification, which is worse than honest HTTP.
- Close :80 on day one — breaks any consumer that hasn't migrated; redirect first, close on evidence.
