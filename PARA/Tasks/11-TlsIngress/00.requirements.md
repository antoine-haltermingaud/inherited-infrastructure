# Requirements — 11-TlsIngress

**Planned for Delta this month. Deliberately NOT implemented in the take-home** — blocked on the domain/DNS decision with the team.

## Acceptance criteria

- Domain decided, ACM certificate issued and DNS-validated (auto-renewing).
- ALB: :443 listener with the cert; :80 becomes a 301 redirect — kept alive until every consumer has moved, then optionally closed.
- All internal consumers inventoried and migrated to the https URL; ALB access logs show :80 traffic at ~zero before any hard cutoff.
- TLS policy: current AWS-recommended (no legacy protocols).

## Constraints

- Redirect-first rollout — never break a consumer that hasn't migrated; the redirect makes migration lazy-safe for GETs.
