# Requirements — 01-InfraReview

Deliverable: `REVIEW.md` at repo root.

## Acceptance criteria

- Findings ranked by priority **for this context**, each with a 1–2 sentence rationale.
- Ranking is defensible: severity x blast radius x likelihood, not a generic checklist order.
- A "reasonable for this context" section naming choices that look odd but are fine, with why.
- A week-one plan: what gets fixed Mon–Fri, and what deliberately waits — with the reason it waits.
- Production migration risk called out where a fix cannot be applied to a live system safely.
- Hand-to-the-team readable: no jargon dumps, no unranked wall of bullets.

## Constraints

- Prioritisation and reasoning carry the most weight; completeness is explicitly secondary.
- Budget ~90 minutes of the 4-hour box.
