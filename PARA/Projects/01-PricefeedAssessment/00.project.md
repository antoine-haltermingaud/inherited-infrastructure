# 01-PricefeedAssessment

**State:** active (since 2026-08-24).

## What

Deliver the "Inherited Infrastructure" take-home: an honest infrastructure assessment of
`pricefeed` — an inherited, in-production AWS CDK + ECS Fargate service — plus a small,
defensible set of fixes.

## Why

The evaluation weights *prioritisation and rationale* above completeness. The output is a
document a real team could act on, not an exhaustive audit.

## Context that drives every decision

Millions of users · multiple deploys per day · a handful of engineers, no ops team ·
zero appetite for downtime · cost-conscious startup. Every finding is ranked against
*this* context, not against a generic best-practice checklist.

## End condition

All of the following pushed to `main`:
1. `REVIEW.md` — ranked findings with rationale + a week-one plan.
2. A commit fixing the top 2–3 findings only, `cdk synth` clean, with a team-grade commit message.
3. `AI-NOTES.md` — how AI was used, including at least one concrete disagreement.

## Constraints

- Hard time box: ~4 hours total. Stopping on time is part of the exercise — an honest
  "with more time I would…" beats an overtime submission.
- No AWS account, no deployment. `npx cdk synth` must pass without credentials.
